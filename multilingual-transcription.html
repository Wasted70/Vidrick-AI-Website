Multilingual Offline AI Transcription

Vidrick AI Transcriber 4.1.9 gives Windows users 57 named transcription-language choices plus Auto-detect inside the same local workflow.

57 named language choices

The selector includes major languages such as English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Arabic, Chinese, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Ukrainian and Vietnamese, along with many additional choices.

Auto-detect

Leave the language on Auto-detect when you want Vidrick to determine the recording language automatically.

Translate to English

Translate to English remains available alongside normal transcription for multilingual recordings.

Still local

The expanded language selector does not change Vidrick's local-first design. Transcription continues to run on the user's Windows PC.
