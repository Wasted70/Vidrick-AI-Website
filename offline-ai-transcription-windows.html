Offline AI Transcription for Windows

Vidrick AI Transcriber is a Windows desktop application for turning supported audio and video files into text using AI processing performed on your computer. It is built for people who want useful transcription features without sending their recordings through a cloud transcription service.

Local transcription on your Windows PC

Transcription is performed locally. After the required components are available, your supported media can be processed on the computer running Vidrick.

Built for multi-speaker recordings

Speaker identification helps separate conversations into labeled speakers, making interviews, meetings, podcasts, and other multi-person recordings easier to review.

Use the hardware you already own

Vidrick supports NVIDIA CUDA GPU acceleration for faster processing on compatible systems, with settings exposed directly in the desktop interface.
