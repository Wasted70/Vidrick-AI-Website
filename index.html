One straightforward workflow

Transcribe. Review. Put it to work.

From a quick conversation to a long interview, keep your work in one place.

01

Choose your recording

Open an audio or video file. Select a language, or use Auto-detect. Let Vidrick identify the speakers, or set the expected count.

02

Review the conversation

We’ve worked hard to improve speaker identification, but no automated system is perfect. Review your transcript and speaker labels, checking names, speaker changes, and important quotes against your recording.

03

Export what you need

Save TXT or Word documents, or SRT/VTT subtitles. Use AI Insights for takeaways and local history to revisit your work.

Getting started: Create an account, activate your trial, and download Vidrick. Internet access is needed for setup, component downloads, and license checks—not for uploading your recordings.

*Personal uses your CPU. Professional adds acceleration on compatible NVIDIA GPUs. Processing time varies with recording length, settings, and hardware.

MEMORY & CPU

Better handling of long recordings

Smaller processing steps and memory-aware CPU settings help manage resources. Transcription models are released before speaker analysis.

SPEAKER LABELS

More consistent voice matching

Updated speaker analysis compares recurring voices across long recordings. Review cues help you focus on uncertain matches before sharing.

PROGRESS

A clearer view of the work

Transcription and speaker analysis show separate progress. When a time estimate is uncertain, Vidrick shows that processing is continuing.

Version 4.3.4 updates the trusted HTTPS certificate list used for licensing, updates, and model downloads, while keeping certificate verification and license protections in place.

On 4.3.1? If the in-app updater is blocked, close Vidrick and install the latest Windows version once to restore updating.
