A quick walkthrough

From recording to a transcript you can use.

Create an account, sign in to activate your trial, then download Vidrick on your Windows PC. Your audio is processed locally; internet access is needed for account setup, downloads, and license checks.

1. Choose your recording

Open an audio or video file, select the language and speaker options, and start transcription. Processing time depends on your recording, settings, and hardware.

2. Review the speaker-labeled transcript

Read through the result in the editor. Check speaker assignments, names, and overlapping speech against your recording, and correct anything that needs attention.

3. Export and put it to work

Export your reviewed transcript as TXT or Word, or use SRT/VTT for subtitles. Start with a short recording to find the settings that work for you.

What’s new in 4.3

Long recordings. More controlled memory use. Clearer progress.

Vidrick 4.3 focuses on long interviews, meetings, and podcasts with more controlled memory use, updated voice matching across a recording, and clearer processing stages. Your transcription and speaker analysis continue to run locally on your Windows PC.

MEMORY

Better handling of long recordings

Smaller processing steps help manage memory during long recordings. Transcription models are released before speaker analysis to reduce how much needs to stay in memory at once.

CPU

Processing that considers your PC

CPU processing takes available memory into account, with more conservative resource use on systems with limited RAM.

VOICES

Actively improved speaker diarization

We’ve refined speaker analysis to compare recurring voices across different parts of a long recording. Choose Automatic, or specify the expected number of speakers. We’re continuing to improve this workflow.

PROGRESS

See which stage is working

Transcription and speaker analysis show their own progress. When a time estimate is uncertain, Vidrick shows that work is continuing.

REVIEW

Clear cues for your final review

Updated review cues highlight uncertain speaker matches in long-recording exports so you can focus on the passages that need attention. Review speaker labels before publishing or attributing important quotes.

DIAGNOSTICS

Optional local performance reports

Choose to save timing and memory measurements to help investigate problems. These files stay on your PC and do not include your recording or transcript text.
