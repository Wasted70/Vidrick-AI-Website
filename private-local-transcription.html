Private, Local Transcription Software

Cloud transcription is convenient, but it requires sending recordings away from the computer where they were created. Vidrick takes a different approach: the transcription workflow is designed around local processing on Windows.

Your recordings stay local

Supported audio and video are processed on your computer rather than uploaded to a transcription cloud.

More than speech-to-text

Speaker processing, conversation reconstruction, summaries, action items and exports are designed to run locally as part of the desktop workflow.

A normal Windows application

Vidrick packages the transcription workflow into a graphical Windows app so you do not need to assemble a command-line transcription stack yourself.
